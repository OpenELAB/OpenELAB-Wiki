# 🚀 OpenELAB Wiki

**Get your hardware working. Build your next project.**

Technical documentation for engineers, researchers, students and makers working with AIoT, robotics and digital fabrication.

Explore product manuals, hardware references, setup instructions and engineering guides—from connecting your first development board to integrating sensors and actuators into a robot.

## Make the most of the documentation

Before following a guide, check your **exact product model and hardware revision**, then confirm the required power supply, connections and software versions.

When asking for technical help, include your product model, operating system, firmware or SDK version, connection details and the complete error message.

Found something missing or outdated? Send us the page link and the correction you suggest.
