# Quectel

Quectel develops wireless communication and positioning modules for connected devices. Its cellular modules and development hardware help integrators add mobile-network connectivity to gateways, embedded computers, and remote equipment.

[Browse Quectel products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brand-quectel)

### Product range

* LTE and 5G cellular modules, including RedCap options
* Module form factors such as Mini PCIe and LCC
* Cellular routers and integration hardware
