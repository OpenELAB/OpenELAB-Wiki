### Introduction

Raspberry Pi Zero WH is a compact single-board computer with built-in wireless connectivity, GPIO pins, and a wide range of applications, enabling users to create versatile and affordable projects in the fields of IoT, education, automation, and more.

Raspberry Pi Zero WH measures only 65mm x 30mm x 5mm, making it highly suitable for integration into various projects. It features built-in 802.11n Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 4.0, enabling wireless network connection and communication between devices. Raspberry Pi Zero WH provides 40 GPIO pins for connecting sensors, actuators, and other electronic components, facilitating the development of interactive hardware projects.

It is equipped with a 1GHz single-core ARM Cortex-A7 processor and 512MB of LPDDR2 SDRAM, providing sufficient computing power and running space. It utilizes a microSD card as the primary storage medium, allowing for the installation of the operating system and storage of data.

### Features

* **Compact Linux Development Board:** Equipped with a 1GHz ARM Cortex-A7 processor supporting Raspberry Pi OS, with a size of only 65mm x 30mm x 5mm
* **Ready-to-use:** Mounted with headers and ready for use
* **Wireless Connection:** Built-in 802.11n Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 4.1
* **40-pins compatible Raspberry Pi:** Compatible with connecting sensors, actuators, and other electronic components
* **Extended Memory:** Utilize 512MB of LPDDR2 SDRAM and a microSD card as the primary storage medium
