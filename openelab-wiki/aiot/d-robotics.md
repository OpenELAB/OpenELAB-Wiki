# D-Robotics

D-Robotics develops robot-computing platforms that combine embedded processing with on-device AI acceleration. Its RDK boards and software resources support developers bringing vision models and intelligent behavior into physical devices.

[Browse D-Robotics products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brands-d-robotics)

### Product range

* RDK X3 and RDK X5 development platforms
* RDK S100 and S100P higher-performance robot-computing kits
* Camera accessories, AI model examples, and robotics software resources
