# EBYTE

EBYTE develops wireless communication modules and industrial IoT hardware. Its broader portfolio covers LoRa, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, and data-transmission devices; the OpenELAB selection currently focuses on the E49 evaluation kit.

[Browse EBYTE products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brand-quectel-1)

### Product range

* LoRa and other sub-GHz wireless modules
* Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Zigbee communication modules
* Evaluation kits and industrial data-transmission hardware
