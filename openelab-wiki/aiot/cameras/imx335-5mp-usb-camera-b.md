### **Introduction**

The IMX335 5MP USB Camera is a high-performance imaging module designed for embedded applications. It provides 2K video recording, wide-angle viewing, and efficient low-light performance, making it ideal for AI, IoT, and robotics projects.

### **Features**

* **5MP Resolution**: Captures high-definition images.
* **175° Wide-Angle Lens**: Suitable for surveillance and panoramic shots.
* **2K Video Recording**: Ensures smooth, high-quality output.
* **Low-Light Sensitivity**: Enhanced visibility with Wide Dynamic Range (WDR).
* **Compatibility**: Works with Raspberry Pi and Jetson Orin kits.

### **Specifications**

* **Sensor**: Sony IMX335
* **Resolution**: 2592 × 1944
* **Formats**: MJPG, YVY2
* **Interface**: USB 2.0
* **Frame Rate**: 30 FPS at 1080p
* **Operating Voltage**: 5V USB Power

### **How to Use**

1. Connect the camera via USB to your computer or embedded board.
2. Install any necessary drivers if required.
3. Configure the video settings for your application through compatible software.
4. Test with Raspberry Pi or Jetson Orin to ensure seamless operation.
