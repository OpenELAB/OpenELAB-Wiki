# SONOFF

SONOFF develops connected switches, sensors, and control devices for smart homes. Its range includes Zigbee adapters and accessories alongside Wi-Fi and other connected products for monitoring and automating everyday equipment.

[Browse SONOFF products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brands-sonoff)

### Product range

* Zigbee USB dongles and coordinator hardware
* Temperature, humidity, motion, and scene-control devices
* Smart switches, relays, and connected wall controls
