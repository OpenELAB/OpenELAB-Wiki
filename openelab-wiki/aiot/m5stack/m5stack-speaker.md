# M5Stack Speaker

### M5Stack Speaker 2 Hat (MAX98357)

***

### Product Link

{% embed url="https://openelab.io/products/m5stack-speaker-2-hat" %}

### Description <a href="#description" id="description"></a>

The `'HAT-SPK2` is a speaker with `M5SticKC PLUS`, built-in `MAX98357 digital audio I2S amplifier IC` and 1W speaker, supporting `16-bit, 32kHz to 192kHz sample rate audio input`, built-in `short circuit protection`' and `over-temperature protection`, suitable for a variety of audio applications.

### Features <a href="#features" id="features"></a>

* HIGH-QUALITY AUDIO OUTPUT: SUPPORTS 16-BIT, 32KHZ TO 192KHZ SAMPLE RATE AUDIO INPUT
* Multiple output modes: including mono and stereo, as well as audio input formats such as I2S, left-justified, and right-justified
* Stability and reliability: Built-in short-circuit protection and over-temperature protection
* Programming platforms: Arduino, uiflow

### Applications <a href="#applications" id="applications"></a>

* Audio speakers
* television set
* Car audio
* Smart speakers
