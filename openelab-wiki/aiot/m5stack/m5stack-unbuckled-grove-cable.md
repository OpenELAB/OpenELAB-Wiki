### Description <a href="#description" id="description"></a>

**GROVE Cable** would be a necessary tool used on M5Stack development, since all PORTs on M5Core are GROVE standard. This GROVE cable, compatible with the GROVE port on M5Core,simply have 4 lines, two for data, one for power,and one for Ground. We've offered alternative length: 5cm, 10cm, 20cm, 50cm, 1m, 2m.
