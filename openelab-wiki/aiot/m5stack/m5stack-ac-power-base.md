### Description <a href="#description" id="description"></a>

**AC POWER BASE** is a `high-performance AC->DC` Buck Converter Module, supports `AC 100 ~ 230V` input. Through the internal buck module, the Base offers a reliable output `DC 5V` and `DC 12V`, dual-channel DC power supply. With a built-in EMC and pulse attenuator, It provides excellent anti-interference and high stability. In addition, the Base increases flexibility for any M5 main controllers' power supply, and it is ideally suited for a wide variety of fields including general electrical engineering applications.

Note: Do not open up or disassemble the product for any reason. This product requires 100 \~ 230V AC input.

### Features <a href="#features" id="features"></a>

* Input Voltage.
  * AC 100 \~ 230V INPUT
* Output Voltage.
  * DC 5V@1A OUTPUT
  * DC 12V@0.3A OUTPUT
* Built-in fuse protection 250V/1A
