### Introduction

Atom PortABC is a Grove port extension base specifically designed for the ATOM series. This innovative product allows users to conveniently connect multiple I2C, UART, and GPIO type UNIT units simultaneously.

With the Atom PortABC, users can enjoy the flexibility of expanding their ATOM-based projects with ease. It offers a range of fixing methods, including suspension, Lego connection, and screw fastening, ensuring secure and versatile installation options.

Whether you're building multi-functional mini-node controllers or exploring complex IoT applications, the Atom PortABC provides the perfect solution for expanding your ATOM series capabilities. With its user-friendly design and compatibility with various UNIT units, the Atom PortABC opens up endless possibilities for creating innovative and customized projects.

### How to Use

* **Connect to M5Stack Core**:
  * Connect the M5Stack ATOMIC PortABC Extension Base to your M5Stack Core using the appropriate port, typically the GROVE port.
* **Connect External Devices**:
  * Use the provided A, B, and C ports to connect external devices. These ports support various interfaces like I2C, GPIO, and UART.
