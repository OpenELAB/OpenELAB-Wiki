### Description <a href="#description" id="description"></a>

**CM4STACK** is an all-in-one micro host based on the **Raspberry Pi CM4 Core Board**, featuring the CM4104032 model with built-in Wi-Fi, 4GB of RAM, and 32GB eMMC. This host includes a wealth of peripherals: a 2.0-inch IPS-LCD touchscreen with a resolution of 320x240, 2 USB3.2-A ports (PCIe + ASM3042), a USB-C (2.0) port with **OTG** and power delivery support, a high-resolution multimedia signal interface, a Gigabit Ethernet RJ45 port, a DC-JACK power input (12V@3A), 2 HY2.0-4P expansion ports for I2C and UART, a 2W speaker powered by an AW88298 16-Bit I2S amplifier, a secure encryption chip (ATECC608B), and an RTC chip (BM8563). The device also includes an active cooling fan and an aluminum heat dissipation frame. It supports **DIN rail, wall mounting, and screw fixing**, making it suitable for industrial automation, smart home, IoT, and other applications.

### Features <a href="#features" id="features"></a>

* High-performance embedded system: Built-in powerful chips and interfaces, equipped with a 2.0-inch LCD screen with touch functionality for high-quality video and image display.
* Multiple interfaces and features: Includes an antenna port, 2W speaker, high-resolution multimedia signal interface, USB-C (2.0) port, USB3.2 port, Gigabit Ethernet port (RJ45), I2C interface, UART, and DC power input, supporting a wide range of peripherals and data transfer methods.
* Security features: Equipped with a secure authentication chip AW32901ID-KEY (ATECC608B) for secure data transmission and storage.
* Clock functionality: Includes an RTC chip BM8563 for real-time clock functions.
* Cooling design: Comes with a heat dissipation base and cooling fan to ensure long-term stable operation.
