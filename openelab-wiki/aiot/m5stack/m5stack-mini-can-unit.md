### Description <a href="#description" id="description"></a>

**Mini CAN Unit**is a compact CAN bus communication module that utilizes the TJA1051T chip, offering excellent high-speed data transmission capabilities. Additionally, it is equipped with electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and electrostatic discharge (ESD) protection mechanisms, ensuring enhanced security and stability for data transmission.

Internally, Mini CAN Unit integrates a DCDC power supply chip, accommodating a wide voltage input range of 9-24V. This provides users with a stable and efficient CAN communication solution. Whether you are involved in automotive electronics, industrial automation, or communication systems, Mini CAN Unit offers a reliable and flexible CAN communication solution. It finds extensive applications in automotive electronics, industrial automation, and communication systems.

No matter if you are developing automotive electronic systems, controlling industrial automation processes, or implementing communication systems, Mini CAN Unit is your dependable choice.

### Features <a href="#features" id="features"></a>

* Using TJA1051T/3 chip
* High Speed Data Transfer Capability
* Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) and electrostatic discharge (ESD) protection mechanisms
* VH3.96 Wide terminal voltage input range (9-24v)
