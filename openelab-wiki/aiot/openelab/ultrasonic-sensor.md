### Introduction

This is a commonly used ultrasonic sensor that detects the presence of an obstacle in front of it and detects the detailed distance between the sensor and the obstacle. The principle is the same as the principle of bat flight, that is, the ultrasonic module sends out a high-frequency ultrasonic signals that cannot be heard by the human body. These ultrasonic signals will be reflected back if they touch an obstacle. After receiving the returned information, the distance between the sensor and the obstacle is calculated by judging the time difference between the transmitted signal and the received signal.
