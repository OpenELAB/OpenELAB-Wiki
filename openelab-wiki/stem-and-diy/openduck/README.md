# 🦆 OpenDuck

OpenDuck is an open-source community project building miniature versions of the Disney BDX droid. The current revision, Open Duck Mini v2, stands about 42 cm tall with its legs extended, is driven by 14 Feetech STS3215-C001 serial bus servos (7.4 V / 19 kg·cm) with a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W onboard. The mechanical design, firmware, and trained walking policies are released under the Apache-2.0 license, and the project is developed with sponsorship from Hugging Face and Pollen Robotics.
