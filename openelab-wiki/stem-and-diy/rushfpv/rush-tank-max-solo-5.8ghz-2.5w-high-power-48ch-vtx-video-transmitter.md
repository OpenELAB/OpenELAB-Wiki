### Introduction

RF amplifier is very sensitive to SWR when the high power output. The antenna must be installed before the vtx can be turned on. otherwise, the VTX amplifier will burn out. Damaged antenna and the coaxial cable may also lead to amplifier failure. In the state of not connected Smartaudio, VTX will keep the lowest power block every time it starts. Do not block the airflow duct of the high-efficiency cooling fan, please use it in a frame with air circulation. The 5V output is for camera use only, please do not connect to the power input and battery, this will cause damage to VTX!
