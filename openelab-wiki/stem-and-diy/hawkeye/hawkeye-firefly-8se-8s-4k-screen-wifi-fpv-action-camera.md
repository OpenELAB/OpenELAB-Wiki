### Introduction

The action camera HawKeye Firefly 8S no distortion version offers six-axis gyro video stabilization that can produce very stable video image without losing any view angle. With the 90 degree lens, the sports camera can shoot more realistic and vivid images without any distortion, restoring the details of the beautiful scene. The equipped processor will provide you with real 4K videos and pictures. What is more, the sports camera supports Bluetooth remote control and external microphone, thus, you can capture photos and videos more easily and clearly.
