### Introduction

Hawkeye has added the very compact "Flight-Master" screen to its range of FPV monitors. The built-in 5.8GHz Channel 48 receiver is very easy to use. The screen has a built-in battery that can be charged via the built-in micro USB port.

These mini FPV screens really comes into their own for quickly testing FPV setups and configuring your quad using the BF OSD without having to put your goggles on. As the screen are self-powered, they can be easily added to a radio transmitter to give the pilots FPV video at their fingertips. They are also great for sharing around amongst curious spectators to give them an idea of what FPV is all about. Finally, these monitors can act as an analogue receiver for the [DJI digital goggles](https://www.flyingtech.co.uk/fpv-camera-gimbals/dji-digital-hd-fpv-goggles) – they include a AV output cable that plugs directly into the DJI goggles.

### Features

* On screen voltage indicator
* On screen timer
* On screen receiving intensity display
* 960 x 240 resolution
* Analogue video output – ideal for adding analogue receiving capability to DJI HD FPV Goggles
* Analogue video input – can be used as a small display screen
