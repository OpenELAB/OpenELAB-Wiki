### Introduction

Gens ACE Lipo Battery with T/XT60 Plug is a lithium battery designed for remote control (RC) models, which is widely used in RC airplanes, helicopters, drones, fixed-wing airplanes, racing cars, boats, and many other model applications.

### Features

* Stable automatic stacking technology enables single-cell capacities of 5000mAh.
* Best raw material and excellent workmanship to fulfill customer needs
* Superior Japan and Korea Lithium Polymer raw materials.
* The battery is with Deans and XT60 plug.
