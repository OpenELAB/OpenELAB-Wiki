# Holybro Power Distribution Board (PDB)

### Holybro Power Distribution Board (PDB)

***

### Product Link

{% embed url="https://openelab.io/products/holybro-power-distribution-board" %}

### Introduction

This power distribution board (PDB) is for PM02, PM02D power modules. Power modules with built-in power distribution (e.g., PM03, PM06, PM07) do not require this PDB.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (586).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Features

* &#x20;Used for PM02, PM02D or similar Power Modules

### Specifications

* Pre-soldered XT60 for power modules and batteries&#x20;
* Rated current: 60A&#x20;
* Maximum current: 120A (<60 seconds)&#x20;
* 45x45mm mounting holes
