### Introduction

This is a wonderful folding frame made from full carbon fiber components. All tubes and plates contain no fiberglass and provide an extremely strong and lightweight flying platform for aerial photography, industry or other uses.

Each frame measures 850mm diagonally motor-to-motor. The arms lock in the upward position with a 5-degree upward angle, allowing for improved handling when carrying additional payload. The folding brackets are constructed of aluminum with metal hardware.

### Features

* &#x20;The arm is up 5 ° force effect to play in place, a larger diameter of 20mm carbon tube.
* Aluminum alloy folding parts, nylon tube clamp (tighter clamp) 8 holes motor mount, compatible with more motors.
* The diagonal wheelbase of the motor mount is about 850mm, and it can support up to 16 inch paddle.
* Center plate specification 245mm\*180mm more space, the center plate middle height of about 35mm.
* The gimbal hanging rod adopts 10\*330mm long carbon tube, which can be freely extended and retracted to the desired length, with the multi-hole gimbal plate.
* The tripod adopts 16mm diameter carbon fiber tube, height 350mm, tripod crossbar 500mm, more grounding area and more stable.
* The tripod adopts double tubes of carbon fiber as support, the bearing capacity is more than 50kg (I actually test, no problem to stand on it)
* Stand connectors using plastic parts, low maintenance costs, this rack regardless of any part of the bad.
