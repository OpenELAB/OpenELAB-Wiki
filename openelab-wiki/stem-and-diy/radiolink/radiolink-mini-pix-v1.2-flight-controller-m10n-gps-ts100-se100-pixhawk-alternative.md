### Introduction

The new Radiolink independently developed the Mini PIX, and the enclosure uses closed protection for the barometer to reduce the airflow interference during the movement and reduce the vibration. At the same time, the software algorithm optimizes the air pressure to maintain the PIXHAWK complete function, which is easy to setup.

### Features

* 【Digital Filter Algorithm】--Radiolink mini pix flight controller design by digital filter algorithm for damped vibration, your drone still have a good performance even though without physical vibration dampener.<br>
* 【Mini Size】--New layout from Pixhawk, only 1.54\*1.54\*0.57in, integrating the full functions of the PIXHAWK, same mini size as F4 FC. Suitable for mini racing drone, fixed wing, helicopter, multirotor and rc boat car.<br>
* 【Stabilization】--Design a shell to reduce interference of airflow during flying, air pressure vent to ensure the accuracy of barometer measurement, built-in barometer sensor to make altitude hold stabilization.<br>
* 【Top Firmware Vision】--with STM32F405VGT6 processor and LPS22HB barometer, you just connect Mission Planner to adjust the PID's to reach your perfect settings.<br>
* 【Easier Setting】--Do not need complicated setting for beginner and professor player, Android USB port to make convenient and simple parameter calibration settings, you can enjoy a good fly experience.

### Specifications

* Connector Port: JST GH connector
* Receiver In: PPM/SBUS
* Transmitter: 6 Oneshot/PWM
* USB Voltage: 5v+-0.3v
* Work Voltage: 5.1v
* Work Temperature Rang: -40\~80°
* Support Models: Fixed wing/Multiple Rotors/helicopter/Drone/Car/Boat
* Mini Pix Size: 3.9\*3.9\*1.4cm/1.54\*1.54\*0.47in
* Weight: 15/0.034Lb
