# Genmitsu

Genmitsu is a desktop CNC product brand offering machines and accessories for digital fabrication. Its range gives makers, teaching labs, and small workshops tools for learning CNC workflows and producing prototype parts.

The two model guides below now include manual chapter maps, the complete observed resource-center link index, a non-moving GRBL inventory, work-zero and probe checks, and a staged dry run. Each keeps its own wiring and accessory scope.

| Model            | Main integration distinction                                                    | Guide coverage                                                                                                  |
