# Genmitsu

Genmitsu is a desktop CNC product brand offering machines and accessories for digital fabrication. Its range gives makers, teaching labs, and small workshops tools for learning CNC workflows and producing prototype parts.

### Popular applications

* Learn CAD/CAM, toolpath generation, and CNC machining.
* Produce wooden, plastic, and compatible non-ferrous prototype parts.
* Create enclosures, fixtures, signs, and educational fabrication projects.

Material capability depends on the machine, spindle, cutter, workholding, and cutting parameters.
