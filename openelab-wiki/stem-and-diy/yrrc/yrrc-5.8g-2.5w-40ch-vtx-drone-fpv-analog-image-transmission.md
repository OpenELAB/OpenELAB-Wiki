### Features

* With a built-in power self-check function, it can quickly inspect whether the image transmission is normal.&#x20;
* It also features a frequency locking function that ensures no interference with other devices during startup.
* The power output is adjustable within a range of 0 to 2.5W, allowing seamless switching across different environments.

### Specifications

* Output Power: 0-25-400-800-1500-2500mW
* Input Voltage: 6-36V
* Parameter Adjustment: Dual Buttons / OSD
* Operating Current:
  * ≤ 450mA @ 12V (up to 450mW)
  * ≤ 850mA @ 12V (above 450mW, up to 2500mW)
* Output Frequency: 40 Channels (A-B-E-F-R)
* Antenna Interface: MMCX
* Mounting Holes: 30.5 x 30.5 mm
* Video Format: NTSC/PAL Auto Detect
* Weight: 12.5g (excluding antenna and power cable)
* Dimensions: 35.5 x 36.5 x 10 mm
