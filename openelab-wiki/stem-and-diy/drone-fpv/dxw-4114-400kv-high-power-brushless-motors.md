### Introduction

High-performance, cost-effective D4114 motor by DXW.The motor has good balance, high pulling force, high appearance value, superior performance, and high efficiency. Uses imported NMB bearings for smooth operation and greater durability. Oxygen-free copper winding, imported steel shaft, high fill rate, and higher efficiency. Single motor pulling force can reach up to 3kg, suitable for pulling lines, sea fishing, aerial photography, and other large field applications. Suitable for most multi-rotor wings with shaft distances of 500-1000mm. 60cm ultra-long wires for convenient use at long shaft distances. More magnetic poles, stronger power, and lower heat generation.
