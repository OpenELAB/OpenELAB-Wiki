# Holybro PM02 V3 Power Module 12S

### Holybro PM02 V3 Power Module 12S

### Product Link

### Introduction

This PM02 Power Module provides a simple way to supply regulated 5.2V to flight controller from the battery while providing current consumption and battery voltage measurements via analog signal through a 6 Pin JST-GH cable. It comes with pre-soldered wires, capacitor, and XT60 connector on both ends, along with shrink tubing on the PCBA for physical protection.

### Features

* Designed to power autopilot flight controller, RC receiver, and APM accessories (GPS, radio)
* Compatible with Pix32, Pixhawk4, Pixhawk4 mini, Pix32v5, and Durandal

### Specifications

* Input Voltage: 2S-12S
* PCB Cont. Current Rating: 60A
* PCB Burst Current Rating: 100A (<60 Sec)
* Max Current Sensing: 120A
* Output: DC 5.2V & 3A Max
* Voltage Divider: 18.182
* Amperes per Volt: 36.364&#x20;
* Weight: 20g

### **Package Includes：**

\- 1x Power Module with XT60 Connectors Board (pre-soldered)\
\- 1x Electrolytic capacito: 220uF 63V (pre-soldered)\
\- 1x Molex 6 Position Connector 15 cm\
\- 1x JST GH 6 Position Connector 15 cm&#x20;
