### Introduction

This Rush FPV Tank SOLO VTX is the latest in ultimate VTX power. The Tank SOLO is equipped with a SmartAudio Control protocol, 48 frequency channels, and full channel calibration. This VTX has 7-36V Input, 5V Output, and an 800mW Max Power level. The Heat Sink design with sturdy aluminum casing is perfect for all of your high-powered FPV needs.
