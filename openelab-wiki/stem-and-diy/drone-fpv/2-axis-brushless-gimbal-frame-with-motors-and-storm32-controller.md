### How to Use

1. &#x20;Remove the vibration dampening balls and open the top cover of the installation plate.
2. &#x20;Mount the gimbal top plate to the bottom of the DJI Phantom, and tighten the screws.
3. &#x20;Reinstall the vibration dampening balls.
4. &#x20;Mount your GOPRO camera and secure it with the strap.
