## Product overview <a href="#overview" id="overview"></a>

The **ELEGOO Centauri Carbon 2 Combo** is a 256 × 256 × 256 mm enclosed FDM printer with a single 350 °C hotend and CANVAS automatic switching between four filament channels. This guide covers assembly, a first PLA print, multicolor slicing, LAN/cloud control, TPU bypass, diagnostics, and repair. Four channels share one nozzle: color changes need cutting, retraction, purging and wiping, so they consume more time and material than a single-color job. [Official model FAQ](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/faq/centauri-carbon-2-combo/), [English manual V2.1, printed pp. 1–21](https://elegoo-supportcenter.oss-us-west-1.aliyuncs.com/Centauri%20Carbon%202%20\(Combo\)-User%20Manual.zip).

## Applications <a href="#recommended-use-cases" id="recommended-use-cases"></a>

Use the CC2 Combo for color-coded enclosures, labels, educational cutaways, product appearance models and prototypes. A single-material profile is often more efficient for brackets, jigs, drill guides, wire-routing fixtures and inspection gauges. The available enclosure and high-temperature hotend also make supported ABS/ASA/PC and reinforced-material trials possible, provided the filament's drying, adhesion and process requirements are met. Printer temperature capability alone does not establish finished-part strength, outdoor suitability or a certified production process.

## Hardware and connections <a href="#hardware-architecture" id="hardware-architecture"></a>

### CoreXY motion platform <a href="#corexy-motion-platform" id="corexy-motion-platform"></a>

The two XY motors act through a coupled belt system. A noisy movement or layer shift can come from belt tension, rail friction, a collision or cable interference; it does not identify a failed motor by itself. The bed travels on the Z system. Check clear motion and the absence of shipping restraints before any homing command. A rigid, flat surface matters both for ringing and for bed-pressure measurements. [Noise diagnosis](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/printer-noise-issue), [leveling error 704](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/errorcode-704).

### High-temperature toolhead <a href="#high-temperature-toolhead" id="high-temperature-toolhead"></a>

The toolhead contains the extruder gearbox, cutter and detection boards, heatbreak fan, model-cooling fan, heater, thermistor and hotend. Its rear communication board and nozzle interface board are separate service items. Check the **CC2** part scope when ordering: an original Centauri Carbon hotend or thermistor can look similar and still be wrong. For PLA-CF/PETG-CF, ELEGOO requires a nozzle diameter of **0.4 mm or greater**, and the supplied PLA/PLA+ sample G-code is not a PLA-CF profile. [Clogged-nozzle guide](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/troubleshooting/clogged-nozzle).

### CANVAS four-color system <a href="#canvas-four-color-system" id="canvas-four-color-system"></a>

Route each spool through its matching **numbered holder and CANVAS inlet**. Holder numbers determine the recycling/retraction geometry; do not swap left/right holders just because a spool fits. Seat a spool fully against its holder stop and check that it rotates without rubbing. The four PTFE outlets at the toolhead hub do not need a prescribed insertion order, but each tube must be inserted vertically and fully locked. These are different requirements: holder numbering matters, hub tube-port order does not. [Manual V2.1, printed pp. 9–14](https://elegoo-supportcenter.oss-us-west-1.aliyuncs.com/Centauri%20Carbon%202%20\(Combo\)-User%20Manual.zip), [hub procedure](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/4-in-1-hub-disassembly-installation).

RFID recognition reads an ELEGOO tag; it does not chemically identify arbitrary filament. Present the tag at the reader until the confirmation tone, assign the detected material to the intended slot, and confirm. Untagged filament works with manually entered brand, material and color. Verify the slicer list after loading. Automatic backup requires another suitable channel containing the intended material and color, rather than an arbitrary spare spool. [Model FAQ](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/faq/centauri-carbon-2-combo/).

**Spool adapter:** the current listing contains an internal inconsistency: its usage notes say spools with a 53–58 mm inner diameter may need an adapter, while its later third-party-spool note says the holder fits that range and larger bores may need one. Assess the actual fit and free rotation. The [manufacturer FAQ](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/faq/centauri-carbon-2-combo/) provides an adapter-model route for oversized or unstable spools; do not assume either listing sentence proves a universal fit.

### Chamber and air management <a href="#chamber-and-air-management" id="chamber-and-air-management"></a>

The thermal cover, controlled grille, chamber fan and filter regulate airflow. Enclosure/airflow control is not a documented independently heated chamber temperature specification. Keep ventilation and access to the rear purge chute clear. The bed may reach 110 °C and the nozzle 350 °C; hands and loose tools must remain outside moving/hot areas. [Manual V2.1](https://elegoo-supportcenter.oss-us-west-1.aliyuncs.com/Centauri%20Carbon%202%20\(Combo\)-User%20Manual.zip), [grille faults 1101](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/errorcode-1101) / [1102](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/errorcode-1102).

### Calibration and monitoring <a href="#calibration-and-monitoring" id="calibration-and-monitoring"></a>

Use **Settings → Calibration → One-click Self-check → Select All → Start** for initial commissioning or after major repairs. The selected items include checks such as input shaping and auto leveling; labels vary with firmware. Repeat leveling after moving the printer, changing/removing the hotend, adjusting the bed, replacing leveling hardware, changing plate thickness/side, or clearing calibration data. A camera or AI alert complements direct first-layer observation. It does not establish adhesion, correct slot mapping or mechanically sound assembly. [Upgrade calibration](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/upgrade-centauri-carbon-2-to-centauri-carbon-2-combo), [blob prevention](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/filament-blob-removal-and-prevention).

## Software and development resources <a href="#software-and-workflow" id="software-and-workflow"></a>

### Which slicer should I use first? <a href="#which-slicer-should-i-use-first" id="which-slicer-should-i-use-first"></a>

Start with **ELEGOO Slicer**, select **Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2** and the installed **0.4 mm nozzle**, then the correct plate side and actual filament. The V2.1 manual depicts this profile name even for the Combo printer; CANVAS materials are added/synchronized separately. Do not select original Centauri Carbon merely because its name is close. Install the appropriate Windows `.exe`, Intel Mac `x86_64.dmg`, or Apple silicon `arm64.dmg`; use the official release assets for other supported packaging rather than inferring an OS package from a filename elsewhere. [Official releases](https://github.com/ELEGOO-3D/ElegooSlicer/releases).

The release endpoint reviewed here identified **v1.5.3.5**, dated 2026-09-03. Its notes add multiple build-plate selection and specifically ask users to check first-layer temperature after selecting a plate. That is a reproducibility reference, not a promise that it will remain the latest version. [Release notes](https://github.com/elegooofficial/ElegooSlicer/releases/tag/v1.5.3.5). OrcaSlicer is named in the listing, but use an exact CC2-compatible profile and verify start/end and material-change behavior before relying on it. This guide does not assert that every generic Orca release has identical device/cloud integration.

### LAN, WAN, Matrix and local backend operation

The exact-model [connection guide](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/how-to-connect-the-printer-using-elegoo-slicer-and-elegoo-matrix-app) describes mutually affecting LAN/WAN modes:

1. **LAN:** on the printer use **Settings → Network** to connect Wi-Fi. Put the computer on the same LAN. Enable **Settings → LAN Only**; optionally set an access code. In Slicer choose **Device → Add Printer**, select the discovered machine and connect; supply the code if enabled.
2. **Manual LAN address:** read the printer's IP in Network settings. In Slicer choose **Devices → Add Printer → Manual Connection → LAN**, enter that address and the optional access code. Expect the printer in the local list. Turning LAN Only off disconnects an existing Slicer LAN session.
3. **Slicer cloud/WAN:** give the printer working internet access and disable LAN Only. Sign into Slicer, find the printer PINCODE at **Settings → Device → Account**, then **Device → Manual Add → WAN** and enter it. Accept **Bind** on the printer's external connection prompt. Enabling LAN Only subsequently disconnects WAN.
4. **Matrix app WAN:** the connection article requires CC2 Combo firmware **V01.03.01.89 or higher** and Matrix **1.0.11 or higher**. Disable LAN Only, open the printer account page, and scan its QR code from **Add Printer** in Matrix; alternatively choose **Manual Add → Centauri Carbon 2 Combo** and enter the PINCODE, usually six uppercase letters. With the same account, a successful Matrix binding appears in Slicer.
5. **Connection-mode conflict:** clicking a previously established Slicer LAN entry while Matrix WAN is active can disconnect Matrix and leave the LAN entry offline. The official recovery is to close Slicer, power-cycle the printer, and reconnect Matrix. Choose the intended mode before changing a job workflow.

The [Slicer backend guide](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/how-to-control-cc2c-by-elegoo-slicer-backend) exposes actual coordinates, XY jog buttons, bed-up/bed-down buttons, individual-axis homing, fans and LED control. Jog increments are **0.1, 1, 10 and 30 mm**. Select a small increment and check clearance first. **Z↑ means bed up; Z↓ means bed down.** The reviewed guide describes the Slicer device/backend interface; it does not specify a standalone browser URL, network port, authenticated REST SDK or arbitrary internet exposure procedure. Do not substitute another printer's web address or API.

### Paint and validate a four-color model

Load the channels and set their type/color on the screen, connect Slicer and use **Sync Filament List**. Select a model and open the **Coloring Tool / Color painting** control (manual shortcut **N**); keys **1/2/3/4** switch the selected CANVAS material. Inspect the sliced result, not just the colored surface. [Coloring guide](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/elegoo-slicer-multi-color-printing-model-coloring-guide).

| Painting mode / control   | What it changes and what to inspect                                                                       |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Circle                    | Surface brush; does not penetrate inside the model.                                                       |
| Sphere                    | All faces intersecting its volume, including hidden and back faces. Check for unintended interior colors. |
| Triangle                  | Individual/adjacent triangles under the cursor; mesh detail limits region boundaries.                     |
| Height range              | A horizontal Z range/layers; inspect transitions at the intended heights.                                 |
| Fill                      | Connected surfaces from the selected area.                                                                |
| Gap Fill                  | Small unpainted gaps left between brush regions.                                                          |
| Section View              | Exposes occluded/internal regions for painting.                                                           |
| Ctrl+Z / Shift+left mouse | Undo painting / erase a painted region.                                                                   |

Keep room for the prime/purge tower and other auxiliaries. Reduce unnecessary color changes by grouping same-color regions and reviewing object orientation. Tune flush volumes using small samples before reducing them on a long print; insufficient purge contaminates colors or leaves an unsuitable material transition. Filament switching does not make every pair of materials mechanically/thermally compatible.

### PLA/PETG support interfaces for flat overhangs

ELEGOO documents a specific workflow for **large, flat overhangs**, using a PLA model and PETG interface; it is not intended as a universal figurine-support preset. Load PLA and PETG into two channels, enter their types/colors and synchronize them. Enable **Normal** supports, choose **PETG** as support/raft interface material, set **Top Z Distance = 0**, and use **Lines** (recommended), Overlapping Lines or Grid interface pattern. Slice and check the toolpath and purge transitions; expect longer printing. Do not generalize zero gap to same-material supports. [Official support-interface procedure](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/how-to-set-a-smooth-support-contact-surface).

### Time-lapse recording and export

This procedure explicitly covers **Centauri Carbon 2 and Carbon 2 Combo**, not Centauri 2/2 Combo. Enable **Settings → LAN Only**, connect Slicer on the same LAN, slice, then **Print → Upload and Print → Timelapse → Print**. For a USB-started print, enable time-lapse manually on the touchscreen. Export the completed video from **Elegoo Slicer → Background → Video List → Export**; the folder icon beside export progress opens its location. The specific guide says cloud-mode camera viewing can work while time-lapse activation is unavailable, so a working remote camera does not establish recording support in that mode. [Time-lapse guide](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/how-to-use-time-lapse-photography).

### Nexprint models through ELEGOO Matrix

For CC2/CC2 Combo, the [Matrix/Nexprint guide](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/c2-series-matrix-app-nest-print-printing) requires current app/printer firmware and an account. Bind the device, open **Model**, filter/search, select a model, choose **Prepare to Print → Filament Selection**, map colors and confirm the loaded parameters before starting. The app can set nozzle/bed targets and expose movement/fan controls and **Pause Printing / Stop Printing** confirmation. Speed selections are **Silent 50%, Balanced 100%, Sport 130%, Ludicrous 160%**; these scale a job's speed, not a guarantee that every toolpath can safely/accurately achieve the headline maximum. Start with Balanced and the intended material profile.

Record the installed version at **Settings → Check for Updates / About Printer** and compare it with the exact-model release offered by the printer or official support. Update when needed for a supported feature or fix, preserve stable profile/calibration records, and complete the prescribed checks afterward. Matrix's minimum version above is one documented feature requirement; it does not establish that an unrelated model's firmware is compatible.

The official [CentauriCarbon2 source repository](https://github.com/elegooofficial/CentauriCarbon2) was reviewed at commit [`5a2ea7fc03e707552701b1a69f463699cbd39230`](https://github.com/elegooofficial/CentauriCarbon2/tree/5a2ea7fc03e707552701b1a69f463699cbd39230). It names `debugfile/printer.cfg`, `printer_dsp.cfg`, `product_test.cfg` and `temperature_sensors.cfg`, an ARM cross-compilation toolchain, CMake, `gcc-arm-none-eabi`, and MCU targets `toolhead_gd32` / `bed_sensor`. Source availability is useful for auditing; it does not establish a complete supported host SDK, recovery image or verified installation procedure. Use released official firmware for routine operation; no firmware build or flash was performed for this guide.

## Getting started <a href="#initial-setup-checklist" id="initial-setup-checklist"></a>

### Assemble and obtain the first PLA print

**Prerequisites:** exact CC2 Combo hardware; supplied correct mains lead; a rigid level table in a 5–40 °C room; clean fully dry build plate; supplied 1.5/2.0/2.5/3.0 mm keys and screwdriver; known PLA/PLA+; USB drive or same-LAN computer; ELEGOO Slicer for a custom model. The illustrated reference is **V2.1, English manual dated 2026-04-16**, inside the [Carbon 2 series manual ZIP](https://elegoo-supportcenter.oss-us-west-1.aliyuncs.com/Centauri%20Carbon%202%20\(Combo\)-User%20Manual.zip), printed pp. 5–20. Do not select the separate Centauri 2 manual ZIP.

1. **Inventory and remove packing.** With power disconnected, remove the top foam, screen and tool kit; open the glass door, remove the thermal cover and its packed foam, wiper assembly and CANVAS module. Remove the **three blue-marked heated-bed transport screws using a 2.5 mm key** (manual printed p. 7). Keep them for transport; do not confuse them with operational bed fasteners.
2. **Mount the screen.** At the screen end, insert the FPC with **gold contacts upward** while using the connector release as illustrated. Align the screen with the chassis slot and press it down into position. Do not force a tilted ribbon or pull on the conductors.
3. **Install the wiper.** Align it to the rear panel. The V2.1 manual specifies **two FM3×12 screws at the top and two PM3×4 at the bottom**. The store package list contains some different fastener labels; use this exact illustrated manual and the physical mating part rather than substituting by appearance.
4. **Install CANVAS and hub.** Remove mounting-hole silicone plugs where present. Fix the CANVAS bracket with **two PM3×50**, the feeding module with **three PM3×8**, and the filament hub with **two PM3×6**. Insert the hub's metal tube downward into the toolhead pneumatic fitting before securing it. Seat all four top CANVAS tubes, route to the hub and fit the tube clips without sharp bends.
5. **Install holders, cable and cover.** Fix the four spool-holder bases using **eight PM3×12** in the manual. Snap each left/right holder into its matching numbered position. Connect the four-pin cable angled end at the printer rear and straight end at CANVAS. Assemble the thermal-cover extension by aligning and sliding it upward until locked, then fit the cover. Keep the purge route clear.
6. **Power on and calibrate.** Confirm packing/tools are removed and the plate is seated. Connect the grounded supply, switch on, select language/region as prompted, and run **Settings → Calibration → One-click Self-check → Select All → Start**. Wait for completion; stop and diagnose an error rather than repeatedly restarting a failed homing or leveling move. The expected checkpoint is successful checks without scraping, an obstruction or an error prompt.
7. **Register and load PLA.** Present an RFID tag and assign its information to the correct slot, or enter the actual brand/type/color manually. Seat the spool against its stop and guide the filament into the matching CANVAS inlet while retaining its free end to avoid tangles. The feeder advances to its ready position. On **Filament → channel → Load**, wait for completion and check normal extrusion. Confirm the toolhead filament icon becomes green and material emerges without prolonged clicking/slipping.
8. **Choose a bounded first file.** Either choose an included **PLA/PLA+** test file in the print-file page's **Local** list, or create a small cube in ELEGOO Slicer with the **Centauri Carbon 2 / 0.4 mm** profile, actual plate side and PLA profile. Review first-layer placement, supports and estimated material before **Slice Plate**. Export **G-code** to USB or send through the LAN procedure. Long-pressing a USB file imports it to local storage according to the manual. Do not use the supplied PLA G-code for TPU or CF material.
9. **Observe and finish.** Confirm the selected channel, nozzle/bed targets and calibration options in the print dialog. Start the job. Stay nearby through at least the first **five layers**, watching for a continuous adherent first layer and correct toolhead motion. If the part lifts, filament wraps around the nozzle or the toolhead collides, stop the print and use the relevant diagnostic section. When complete, allow the part/plate to cool, remove the flexible plate and bend gently to release the model; do not permanently over-bend it.

**Expected result:** a small PLA/PLA+ part completes with a continuous attached first layer, no feeding/heating/leveling error, and clean normal extrusion. This is an expected manufacturer-supported checkpoint, **not a print performed during this documentation work**. If USB succeeds while remote transfer fails, keep the same G-code and diagnose the connection rather than changing mechanics and networking simultaneously. Save the printer/slicer versions, plate side, nozzle, material, temperatures and outcome with the print record.

### How should TPU be handled? <a href="#how-should-tpu-be-handled" id="how-should-tpu-be-handled"></a>

Print the [official flexible-filament adapter STL](https://wiki.elegoo.com/cc2c/how-to-print-tpu/flexible_filament_printing_adapter.stl) before disabling CANVAS. Prepare **three PTFE couplers/collets, three locking clips**, and two pieces of **4 mm OD / 2 mm ID PTFE**, **48 mm** and **25 mm** long. Follow the [illustrated TPU article](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/how-to-print-tpu) for the adapter's port positions.

1. Switch off and unplug. Disconnect the CANVAS communication cable. Release both upper/lower tubes at **channel 1** by depressing their push-fit collars; do not pull against a locked collar.
2. Fit the three collets into the adapter and the **48 mm** tube in its central port. Clip the adapter to CANVAS with its upper groove on the upper raised feature and lower slot aligned to channel 1's inlet.
3. Fit the original long upper channel-1 tube to the adapter's upper port. Fit the **25 mm** piece in its lower port and restore the original short channel-1 tube at its original location, as pictured. Install the three locking clips and gently pull each tube to confirm retention.
4. Insert TPU through the lower adapter path toward the hub/toolhead until it reaches the detection point. After restoring power, the toolhead icon should indicate filament in green. Set **Filament → Edit → TPU → Confirm**, then **Load TPU** and check nozzle extrusion.
5. In Slicer set **channel 1 = TPU**, slice with the appropriate profile and use **Slice Plate → Print → Upload and Switch to Printer Page / Upload and Print → Print** as needed. This is a single flexible-material workflow, not automatic four-channel TPU switching.

**Expected behavior:** TPU extrudes normally through the bypass. The official article notes a **35 °C default TPU bed target**, whereas auto leveling defaults to **60 °C**; cooling from leveling to TPU's target can take about **15–25 minutes**. That wait is not automatically a stalled printer. Do not shorten it by changing temperature without evaluating the intended recipe.

### Upgrade a Carbon 2 to a Carbon 2 Combo

This is a retrofit for an identified **Centauri Carbon 2**, not a firmware conversion of an original Centauri Carbon or proof that a different Centauri 2 kit fits. The purchased Combo already has these components. The [upgrade article](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/upgrade-centauri-carbon-2-to-centauri-carbon-2-combo) lists hub, four PTFE tubes/fixing block, thermal cover, CANVAS/module bracket, filament-hub bracket, four holder bases/holders and four-pin cable; tools are 2.0/2.5/3.0 mm keys and Phillips driver.

With power disconnected, remove and retain the original side spool holder/fixing block and detector cable, original feed tube and glass top; remove the mounting silicone plugs. Mount CANVAS bracket with **2×PM3×50**, module **3×PM3×8**, hub bracket at the toolhead with **2×PM3×14**, hub **2×PM3×6**, and four holder bases **8×PM3×12**. Fit numbered holders, tubes/fixing block, communication cable and assembled thermal cover. Only after complete assembly restore power: firmware should detect the added CANVAS and enable four-color capability. Run **Settings → Calibration → One-click Self-check → Select All → Start** and validate a small PLA multicolor job. The illustrated [official upgrade video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Yh7CshvNF4) accompanies the procedure.

## Troubleshooting and FAQs <a href="#troubleshooting-guide" id="troubleshooting-guide"></a>

### Find the failing stage before replacing a part

Record the exact error, material and channel, whether it occurs during loading, heating, homing, leveling, a color change or steady printing, and whether it repeats from the same USB G-code. For connectors, covers and board work: **cool the machine, switch off and unplug before disconnection**; restore wiring/covers before a functional test. The heater/bed/power articles include exposed powered tests. Those are service diagnostics, not prerequisites for a first print. Use the original diagrams and qualified service for live AC measurements rather than deriving terminals from wire color alone.

| Symptom                                         | Ordered checks and useful next result                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First layer fails / local thin or thick regions | Clean and fully dry the plate; seat it flat; check correct side/profile and first-layer targets; auto-level again. If only one region remains wrong, inspect bed tilt and pressure hardware using the leveling procedure. |
| Stringing / blobs                               | Verify actual material/profile and drying, then temperature/retraction; inspect the first layer and supported geometry. Do not reduce temperature blindly if the real problem is incomplete melting.                      |
| Under-extrusion                                 | Check freely rotating spool and tube/hub resistance, correct diameter/profile, nozzle/fan condition and gearbox grip, in that order.                                                                                      |
| Warping / layer shift                           | Check adhesion and lifted edges first; nozzle collisions can shift layers. Then inspect cable clearance, free movement, belt tension, speed/acceleration and a stable table.                                              |
| Wrong color                                     | Synchronize actual channel materials/colors, inspect model painting and sliced preview; print a small mapping sample before the full part.                                                                                |
| File or USB not recognized                      | Re-export G-code using the correct CC2/nozzle profile. Try a reliable small FAT32 drive, a short plain filename and root-directory placement. Logs specifically require ≤16 GB.                                           |

These routes synthesize the [exact-model FAQ](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/faq/centauri-carbon-2-combo/) and detailed articles below. A code is a diagnostic starting point, not proof that every named board must be replaced.

### Isolate a stuck load or clogged nozzle

Use the [load-is-stuck article](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/troubleshooting/filament-load-is-stuck), [clog diagnosis](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/troubleshooting/clogged-nozzle) and newer [unclogging procedure](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/unclogging-the-nozzle) together:

1. Check correctly numbered holders, spool seating/rotation, tangles and a clean undamaged filament end. Ordinary CANVAS feeding does not support TPU. The tubes are 2 mm ID; an oversized/deformed section can jam. Compare several diameter measurements or a known-good spool.
2. Unplug before removing tubes/hub. Depress collars, pull tubes straight, remove broken fragments and check hub passage. If the same error occurs on every channel, focus downstream rather than replacing four feeders.
3. Check room temperature (5–40 °C), correct filament/nozzle profile and heatbreak fan. Verify fan operation at the documented 50 °C test target after reassembly. Heat creep and high-temperature residue can mimic a nozzle obstruction.
4. If gearbox diagnosis is necessary, prepare the lowered hotend with power disconnected, keeping its heater/thermistor wires intact and connected, and support it clear of surrounding parts. The **stuck-load article, Step 6**, then requires **Control → Prepare → set nozzle to 170 °C → wait until it reaches 170 °C → ▽** to check whether the gearbox feeds filament through. The older clogged-nozzle article describes a similar feed check without this numerical target. Keep the 170 °C stuck-load variant separate from the preceding **50 °C fan check** and from material printing temperatures. **Do not use Filament → Load/Unload**: it can home the head while the hotend is unsecured and damage the plate/wiring. Unplug again before tension adjustment or opening the gearbox. Counterclockwise increases spring grip; adjust incrementally, not to maximum pressure.
5. Clear loose gearbox fragments, inspect drive teeth and manually verify passage with a short filament piece. Keep cutter/spring parts under control. Fully secure the hotend and restore connectors before normal loading/heating.
6. For a worn nozzle tip, replace the correct assembly instead of repeatedly unclogging. Otherwise the newer guide tries a temperature slightly above the material's normal setting (PLA example **210 → 220–230 °C**), then extrusion. Keep the door closed and face outside the enclosure against splashed molten filament.
7. With heat-resistant gloves, use a cleaning needle vertically from the nozzle underside, gently up/down without sideways force. If the needle cannot enter or extrusion remains completely blocked, stop and replace/service the hotend.
8. The newer cold-pull procedure is for a **partial** clog with extrusion still possible: use PLA at its normal print temperature, extrude a little, cool to **70 °C**, retract using **Control → Prepare → △**, then inspect the tip and repeat if contaminated. The older clogged-nozzle article says **100 °C**; follow the newer dedicated procedure's conditions rather than mixing both temperatures. Stop if no extrusion is possible or the filament cannot be removed without excessive force.
9. Reassemble and verify extrusion, then **Settings → Calibration → Auto Leveling** before a small print.

### Purge at the wrong location or cover detachment

For waste missing the chute, unplug and inspect the wiper swing arm/spring return; remove its cover to clear debris if needed. Move the toolhead gently to Y-Max and check the stopper reaches the frame; an incorrectly routed cable sleeve can prevent proper homing. For belt adjustment, loosen the **four XY tensioner screws**, move the head through XY to redistribute automatic tension, and retighten. [Wiper/purge diagnosis](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/nozzle-wiper-kit-malfunction-nozzle-purges-at-Incorrect-position-during-filament-unloading).

For a falling front cover, check its seating and magnets, then wiper collision and first-layer lifting. The support root links an [older Centauri Carbon front-cover article](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/Centauri-carbon/tool-head-front-cover-detachment); retain its cause-finding logic with the **CC2-specific 707, wiper and leveling procedures**, rather than treating its older repair links as CC2 part approval. It describes a **230 × 230 × 0.2 mm** single-layer cube test (set a 230 mm cube, disable uniform scale and set Z to 0.2 mm). Use that diagnostic only after a small print and normal leveling are established. Record G-code/STL, label, first-layer photos, magnet photos, firmware and SN for persistent failures.

### Filament blob removal and prevention

A detached model can be dragged by the nozzle while filament continues extruding, forming a blob. Stop early when adhesion fails. First inspect heater/thermistor leads: damaged wires mean **do not energize the hotend**. The [official blob guide](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/filament-blob-removal-and-prevention) uses intact PLA heating at **220 °C for 3–5 minutes**, then switches the machine off before gently lifting softened material with pliers/tweezers; protect wires and wear heat-resistant gloves. Other materials use their own recommended temperature plus the guide's 10–20 °C margin within the printer/material limits. Remove/clean the silicone sock after safe access.

If a blob entraps broken heater wiring, ELEGOO describes staged removal using an external soldering iron **above 300 °C** with the printer off; this is specialist hotend salvage, requiring ventilation and avoidance of every wire/plastic part. Do not pry a solid blob against fragile sensor leads. Replace damaged heater/thermistor or a leaking hotend. Repeat leveling after hotend removal and **PID calibration when heater/thermistor components were replaced**.

Prevent recurrence by washing/drying the plate, avoiding oily fingerprints, checking actual material/profile, placing the model flat (**Place on Face / Auto Arrange**), previewing the first-layer path, adding sufficient supports and brim where contact area is small, and observing the first five layers. Check for leakage above the nozzle rather than repeatedly cleaning a defective assembly.

### Manual bed leveling when automatic leveling is not enough

Use this for a tilted bed, uneven regional first layer or abnormal leveling, after plate cleanliness/installation and table stability are addressed. Tools: **2.0 mm key, A4 paper and pliers**. The [manual-leveling procedure](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/how-to-manually-level-the-heated-bed) is a mechanical reset, not a first response to every adhesion defect.

1. Switch off/unplug; remove the ten bottom-cover screws. Loosen the two Z timing-belt tensioner screws, remove its spring, and release the upper-left Z belt as illustrated.
2. Align all **three Z fixing blocks flush with the chassis bottom**; restore belt, tensioner and spring. Keep access until the manual adjustment is complete.
3. With the printer supported safely on its side as the source shows, pull the Z belt manually to raise the bed. Put A4 paper under the nozzle at the center and adjust for slight drag without scratching.
4. Check each corner, adjusting the bed lock nuts and repeat **2–3 passes**. **Clockwise increases nozzle/bed separation; counterclockwise reduces it** in this mechanism. Move the head gently while checking paper drag; stop if paper locks rather than scraping the plate.
5. Restore all removed parts and close the bottom cover before powered operation. Run **Settings → Calibration → Auto Leveling** and inspect a first-layer sample. Continued pressing at a corner needs pressure-sensor/adapter diagnosis, not repeated forceful leveling.

### Frequently asked compatibility questions <a href="#faq" id="faq"></a>

#### Is the Centauri Carbon 2 Combo only for multicolor printing? <a href="#is-the-centauri-carbon-2-combo-only-for-multicolor-printing" id="is-the-centauri-carbon-2-combo-only-for-multicolor-printing"></a>

No. Select one actual channel and a suitable single-material profile for functional parts. CANVAS adds switching/backup convenience; it does not require a purge-heavy four-color project for every job. [Model FAQ](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/faq/centauri-carbon-2-combo/).

#### Can it print carbon-fiber filaments? <a href="#can-it-print-carbon-fiber-filaments" id="can-it-print-carbon-fiber-filaments"></a>

The current manufacturer FAQ lists PLA-CF and PETG-CF. Use the matching CC2 nozzle of **0.4 mm or larger**, dry filament and its own profile, and inspect wear in the nozzle, gears, tubes and CANVAS. Material names are not interchangeable: the baseline's PET-CF mention does not supply a PET-CF process recipe. [Clogged-nozzle/material guidance](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/troubleshooting/clogged-nozzle).

#### Does the built-in filtration make ABS or ASA printing safe indoors? <a href="#does-the-built-in-filtration-make-abs-or-asa-printing-safe-indoors" id="does-the-built-in-filtration-make-abs-or-asa-printing-safe-indoors"></a>

The printer includes HEPA/activated-carbon filtration, but the reviewed documents do not establish a quantified exposure limit or a sealed-room safety guarantee. Use appropriate room ventilation and material handling, monitor filter condition and replace it/reset the reminder as directed. [High-temperature FAQ](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/faq/centauri-carbon-2-combo/), [filter reminder 109](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/errorcode-109).

## Maintenance and replacement procedures <a href="#best-practices" id="best-practices"></a>

### Routine cleaning, lubrication and inspection

The [CC2 maintenance guide](https://wiki.elegoo.com/en/centauri-carbon-2-combo/printer-maintenance-guide) calls for dish detergent/alcohol, a lint-free cloth, soft brush and **lithium-based white grease**. Wash both PEI surfaces with dish soap/water and sponge, dry completely, avoid metal brushes and fingerprints. Clean XY shafts and Z shaft/leadscrew, apply a small even lubricant film, then distribute it with movement; handle cleaning/lubrication unplugged and restore covers before touchscreen Z movement. Do not spray grease into sensors or coat the plate.

Clear gearbox/feeding-module filament debris with a soft brush; replace visibly worn gears. Lubricate only the gear areas designated by the source figures—especially during gear-set replacement, the article marks a specific area and excludes the rest. For stretched XY belts, loosen the **four tensioner screws**, move the head through XY and retighten after tension redistributes. Inspect nozzle, wiper, cutter, tube retention, fans, cable sleeves and grille, especially after abrasive filament. No single hours-based interval is stated for all these parts; the filter article separately recommends three months.

### Repair preparation and functional return

If the printer still homes normally, use its controls to move the bed to the working position requested by the specific article **before** shutdown. If homing itself is faulty, do not force that preparation step. Cool/unplug, photograph and label connections, retain screw positions/lengths, release latches and support loose covers/modules. Ten screws commonly hold the bottom cover and sixteen the back, but cable-replacement and CANVAS articles show differing counts/batches; never force a panel because a generic count says all screws are out.

The following procedures summarize the actual disassembly sequence, distinctive installation requirements and return check in each source. Open its linked illustrations before removing a part: routing, clip location and screw-length diagrams are integral to the repair. No screw torque is invented here.

### Known limitations and risk notes <a href="#known-limitations-and-risk-notes" id="known-limitations-and-risk-notes"></a>

The guide supplies source-supported procedures, not a tested firmware image or universal material preset. Retain separate records for nozzle/plate, drying, material lot, profile and calibration. Multicolor purge waste and longer jobs are inherent in single-nozzle switching; maximum speed must be constrained by the chosen process. A factory reset, model-upgrade selection or deep rebuild can change configuration; record the prior state. Official articles include some conflicting wording and batch-dependent hardware, identified beside the relevant procedure rather than silently normalized.
