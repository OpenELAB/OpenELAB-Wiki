# WHEELTEC

WHEELTEC develops educational robotics platforms, control-system kits, and robot components. Its range spans PID and balancing experiments through ROS-based mobile robots, making it useful for learning both control fundamentals and autonomous-system integration.

[Browse WHEELTEC products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brands-wheeltec)

### Product range

* PID, inverted-pendulum, and balance-control learning kits
* Wheeled chassis, ROS robot cars, and mobile manipulators
* Vision modules, ranging sensors, motor hardware, and robot accessories

### Popular applications

* Teach feedback control, stabilization, and motor tuning.
* Develop ROS 2 mapping, navigation, and mobile manipulation.
* Build line-following, balance, and sensor-fusion experiments.
