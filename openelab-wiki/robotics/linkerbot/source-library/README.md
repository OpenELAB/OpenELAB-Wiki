## Reading and safe use

Page numbers refer to the original PDF file, including the cover and front matter. Images preserve diagrams and table layout; extracted text preserves the supplied wording but may read columns out of order. Download the PDF parts for native vector drawings and original page contents. Parts collectively contain every page in order. Original manufacturer wording and discrepancies are retained, with explanatory notes in the model guides.

The L30 Standard manual covers the tendon-driven model, including its wrist. Its store/manual voltage and bus conflict blocks powered commissioning of an unconfirmed variant. L20 and O6 voltage ranges require the supplied adapter and actual unit labeling; the L20 connector is marked 24 V. O20/O30 store links remain unverified. These references do not establish a safe payload, certified emergency stop or universal SDK compatibility.
