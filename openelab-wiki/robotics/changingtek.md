# ChangingTek

ChangingTek develops robotic grippers and manipulation hardware. Its range includes parallel and adaptive end effectors, biomimetic hands, and handheld data-collection devices for industrial automation and robotics research.

[Browse ChangingTek products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brands-changingtek)

### Product range

* CTPM parallel two-finger and three-finger electric grippers
* CTAG adaptive and collaborative grippers and robotic hands
* Handheld gripper tools for teleoperation and data collection
