# SLAMTEC

SLAMTEC develops LiDAR and localization products for mobile robotics. Its ecosystem spans RPLIDAR scanners, mapping modules, and visual-inertial spatial sensors for developers building navigation and perception systems.

[Browse SLAMTEC products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brands-slamtec)

### Product range

* RPLIDAR 360-degree 2D laser scanners
* Mapper localization and mapping modules
* Aurora visual-inertial perception systems and industrial LiDAR
