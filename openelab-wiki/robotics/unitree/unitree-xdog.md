# Unitree XDog: historical quadruped prototype reference

{% hint style="info" %}
**Historical directory reference: XDog research prototype.** This is not an observed retail product, developer kit or OpenELAB SKU. No store URL, commissioning readiness or current support entitlement is claimed. **No verified OpenELAB listing or SKU.**

XDog is the quadruped research prototype described in Unitree's company history before Laikago. This short reference preserves its place in the manufacturer timeline and directs engineering readers to the actual historical sources. [Official company history](https://www.unitree.com/about/)

## Product overview <a href="#product-overview" id="product-overview"></a>

The English history places the research during **2013–2016** and describes low-cost outer-rotor brushless permanent-magnet motors, motor-drive boards, software, master/slave control and single-leg testing. A Go1-page timeline uses **2013–2015** for XDog. Preserve those source-specific periods; neither establishes a retail launch date. Company formation in 2016 and the reconstructed Laikago platform in 2017 are later events. [Company history](https://www.unitree.com/about/), [Go1 timeline](https://www.unitree.com/go1)
