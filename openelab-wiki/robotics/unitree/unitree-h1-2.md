# Unitree H1-2: 27-joint hardware, parallel ankles and HG state telemetry

{% hint style="info" %}
[**Unitree H1-2 — verified OpenELAB product listing**](https://openelab.io/products/unitree-h1-2-humanoid-robot) · SKU `Unitree-H1-2` · observed catalog has one Default Title option (product `8846453145798`, variant `45731749658822`). This identifies the model, not a guaranteed hand, computer, battery or firmware bundle.
{% endhint %}

Unitree H1-2 is a full-size humanoid research platform. This guide covers its own hardware, operating manual and a conditional **state-only** SDK2 checkpoint. H1-2 has 27 active body joints and uses the HG DDS message family; H1’s GO decoder and 19-joint map are incompatible. See the separate [H1 guide](unitree-h1.md) for the other robot.

## Product overview <a href="#product-overview" id="product-overview"></a>

H1-2 combines a torso computer, depth/laser sensing, articulated legs and arms, a waist joint and two removable battery packs. Unitree’s [current H1/H1-2 comparative product table](https://www.unitree.com/h1/) and [model-specific overview diagram](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2025/4/18/15354a6e499f44ef8c4ec152d2e0bd9b_2481x3509.png) describe the platform. H1-2 adds two-axis ankles, three-axis wrists and a larger interface board; its approximately 70 kg body is not the 47 kg H1.

The audited catalog contains exactly one H1-2 SKU and no separate options for development entitlement, hands, compute, chargers or firmware. A supplier-confirmed serial-number/BOM and controller revision are prerequisites for selecting accessories and commissioning. The manuals describe development functions, but they do not certify that every retail unit exposes the same network/API/control permissions.

## Applications <a href="#applications" id="applications"></a>

Use the documented platform for supervised locomotion research, perception integration, state logging and, after a separate approved commissioning process, controller development. State logging is the smallest software result here: receiving one correctly decoded message makes model/interface errors visible before any custom command publisher exists.

The wrist/parallel-ankle design supports research into additional articulation, but grasping depends on the delivered optional hand and its own interface. The H1-2 remote V1.0 explicitly excludes sitting and dancing; current stairs are unsupported. Do not import H1 routines. Neither manual establishes unattended operation, person-carrying, collaborative safety certification or a safe proximity envelope around moving joints. Operators must be at least 18 and trained on the exact configuration. Keep line of sight, a prepared frame and the model’s operating clearances.

### Battery, charger and controller handling <a href="#battery-charger-and-controller-handling" id="battery-charger-and-controller-handling"></a>

The H1-2 user manual independently specifies **removed, switched-off packs**, the original matching charger, AC nameplate matching before connection, AC first then battery, cooling to room temperature before charging, 1 Hz charge flashing and pack LEDs off when full ([charging connection](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2025/4/18/02661ab19f4344778beb39c02e97d92f_2481x3509.png), [indications](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2025/4/18/1e6b89d618b0451daa63fc8c61da3b09_2481x3508.png)). Its own storage instructions say avoid above 40 °C and restore to about 70% if below 30% ([maintenance/revision page](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2025/4/18/0b0f39b3f6cc4b088eef831b4fca8a8e_2481x3508.png)).

An H1-2-specific battery/charger electrical manual or explicit H1 accessory compatibility statement was not supplied. Accordingly, H1’s 28.8 V/33.6 V/9 A accessory values, protection thresholds, dimensions and >15 s forced cutoff are **not** H1-2 specifications. Confirm both pack IDs, charger output/nameplate, allowed pair and exact power-off sequence before charging or commissioning. The normal shutdown paragraph (scan 18) repeats “press and hold” ambiguously. Its own remote-failure section (scan 20) separately specifies a brief press of both pack buttons together, then a hold longer than 2 s, only with the robot suspended, feet clear and at least 2 m clearance. This is H1-2 evidence for that bounded fault procedure, not an imported H1 battery instruction; confirm the normal shutdown wording with the supplier.

The exact-model remote manual covers **R3 and older R1**; identify the physical remote first. R3: 780 mAh, nominal about 5 h, listed 5 V/700 mA supply parameter, with off-state USB-C charging from 5 V/2 A; full-charge LEDs are **all on**. R1: 2500 mAh, about 4.5 h, 5 V/2 A, and full-charge LEDs **all off**. Both quote >100 m in an open environment, not a guaranteed indoor link. See [R3 parameters](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2025/4/18/ecedf4a2799e4123afba81fa3c541e98_2482x3509.png), [R3 charging/binding](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2025/4/18/95c2dcceefe34848abb51cb1e7e1b0a8_2481x3509.png), [R1 parameters](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2025/4/18/6b6361647e174a0e93a84e3f1e0b6ba5_2481x3509.png) and [R1 charging](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2025/4/18/68605c8deead42f0b04da3da5de961c2_2481x3509.png). These accessory values are supported by this model’s own remote document.

For stick drift, the remote document uses F1+F3 while sticks are untouched, then full-range stick movement and F3 to save after beeping stops. Calibrate the remote with the robot safely supported and no active motion command path. R3 feedback is changed by triple F3 (sound/vibration) and triple F1 (on/off); holding F1 during shutdown saves the preference. An unconnected, idle R3 may turn off after ten minutes. Remote sound, link LEDs and battery LEDs describe different conditions.

## Getting started <a href="#getting-started" id="getting-started"></a>

### First result: one model-specific state message, conditionally <a href="#first-result-one-model-specific-state-message-conditionally" id="first-result-one-model-specific-state-message-conditionally"></a>

This workflow is for an already authorized development unit with an existing approved SDK2 host environment. It is not permission to install software, flash firmware, wire a custom harness or energize an unsupported robot. If the edition/firmware/network contract is not confirmed, stop after the physical/document identity checkpoint; the draft remains useful without claiming telemetry was obtained.

1. Record `Unitree-H1-2`, robot serial, exact pack/charger IDs, remote R1/R3 revision, installed computers/sensors/hands, firmware and current controller ownership. Confirm this is H1-2, not the other H1-family robot. Resolve the charger/power-off instruction gap before charging or power-on.
2. Have a trained operator prepare the approved protective frame with secured base, both shoulder attachments and feet clear. Inspect connectors, latches, foot rubber, optics and fans, and establish the exact startup reference posture from the linked diagram. Use a dry 0–40 °C, flat/high-traction, observed work area with at least 2 m clearance. Clear personnel before energizing.
3. With two correctly fitted off packs, the own-model startup procedure is a brief press of both power buttons together followed by holding both for more than 2 s. Allow about 120 s initialization and a further 30 s after ankle-limit action. Initialization itself moves joints. Stay suspended under the approved operator procedure; do not enter Ready/Motion or any diagnostic for this result.
4. Verify existing controls before networking. Use **Unitree Explore**, the enterprise account supplied by Unitree, nearby Bluetooth, `Home → Add Robot`, then robot settings for AP/router Wi-Fi and remote settings. Bind the actual remote ID, R3 right DL or R1 left link indicators. The status subheading says “Go App” in the user manual, but the instructions say Explore; confirm the delivered app revision if the UI differs. View available motor/battery status without using gait buttons. App visibility is not proof of DDS access.
5. Have the responsible engineer confirm the physical user-facing Ethernet route, approved host address/subnet and existing SDK environment, and confirm that **domain 0**, `rt/lowstate` and the `HG` schema are valid on this firmware/edition. Use the supplied approved cable only; the circular power-bearing Ethernet positions are not generic RJ45 pinouts. Identify the robot-facing host interface locally with `ip -br addr` on the approved Linux host; substitute its actual name below. Do not change routes/interfaces or try a guessed address as part of this guide.
6. Save the original code below as `unitree-h1-2-state-once.py` in that already prepared environment. The conditional invocation is `python3 unitree-h1-2-state-once.py ROBOT_INTERFACE`, where `ROBOT_INTERFACE` must be replaced by the confirmed local interface. It reads once with a 10 s timeout and closes the subscriber; no mode transition or command publisher is created.

```python
# Original, bounded H1-2 reader; not a vendor motion example.
# Run only after exact firmware/edition/network approval and physical preparation.
import json
import sys

ACTIVE = {0: 'LeftHipYaw', 1: 'LeftHipPitch', 2: 'LeftHipRoll', 3: 'LeftKnee', 4: 'LeftAnklePitch', 5: 'LeftAnkleRoll', 6: 'RightHipYaw', 7: 'RightHipPitch', 8: 'RightHipRoll', 9: 'RightKnee', 10: 'RightAnklePitch', 11: 'RightAnkleRoll', 12: 'WaistYaw', 13: 'LeftShoulderPitch', 14: 'LeftShoulderRoll', 15: 'LeftShoulderYaw', 16: 'LeftElbow', 17: 'LeftWristRoll', 18: 'LeftWristPitch', 19: 'LeftWristYaw', 20: 'RightShoulderPitch', 21: 'RightShoulderRoll', 22: 'RightShoulderYaw', 23: 'RightElbow', 24: 'RightWristRoll', 25: 'RightWristPitch', 26: 'RightWristYaw'}

def main(argv=None):
    args = sys.argv[1:] if argv is None else argv
    if len(args) != 1 or not args[0].strip():
        raise SystemExit("Supply one confirmed robot-facing network interface")
    from unitree_sdk2py.core.channel import ChannelFactoryInitialize, ChannelSubscriber
    from unitree_sdk2py.idl.unitree_hg.msg.dds_ import LowState_
    ChannelFactoryInitialize(0, args[0])  # 0 is the pinned example domain, not universal.
    sub = ChannelSubscriber("rt/lowstate", LowState_)
    try:
        sub.Init()  # no callback: use one bounded synchronous read
        msg = sub.Read(10.0)
        if msg is None:
            raise SystemExit("No state in 10 s; check the approved network/schema, do not change control mode")
        if len(msg.motor_state) < 27:
            raise SystemExit("Unexpected motor_state length; stop and recheck model/schema")
        result = {"tick": msg.tick, "mode_pr": msg.mode_pr, "mode_machine": msg.mode_machine,
                  "joints": {name: {"slot": i, "q_raw": msg.motor_state[i].q,
                                     "dq_raw": msg.motor_state[i].dq}
                             for i, name in ACTIVE.items()}}
        print(json.dumps(result, sort_keys=True))
    finally:
        sub.Close()

if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
```

**Expected result:** one JSON object with `tick`, `mode_pr`, `mode_machine` and 27 named active-joint entries containing source slot and raw `q`/`dq` values. The HG message has 35 array slots but only the mapped 27 are reported; no battery voltage field is fabricated. The code is an original integration example; it has not been executed against CycloneDDS, the SDK or a robot. A decoded packet alone does not prove freshness, full sensor health, correct calibration, remote stop authority or permission to move. Record the actual firmware/schema alongside any later log, and stop on wrong model, stale/implausible data or unexpected array shape.

**Diagnostic checkpoint:** if nothing arrives within 10 s, preserve controller state and check the confirmed interface/subnet, approved network path, domain, firmware/edition telemetry permission and exact schema in that order. Do not release a controller, send a pose or invoke Develop/diagnostic mode to make a subscriber succeed. Closing a subscriber only stops observation; it does not stop or shut down the robot.

### Controls, shutdown and recovery bounds <a href="#controls-shutdown-and-recovery-bounds" id="controls-shutdown-and-recovery-bounds"></a>

The [original current remote key table](https://doc-cdn.unitree.com/static/2025/4/18/282e5f1d1d5b45d5b3afe372d8bddca3_2481x3509.png) distinguishes damping (`L2+B`), ready from damping (`L2+UP`), motion from ready (`R2+X`), zero torque from damping (`L2+Y`) and Develop (`L2+R2`). Ready changes posture; zero torque removes active support; standing may still step if disturbed. In motion, X/Y decrease/increase standing height, A/B decrease/increase leg lift, and START changes continuous walking/standing. These are explanations for operator identification, **not steps in the subscriber workflow**.

`L2+A` in Develop invokes diagnostic position motion and `L2+B` ends it when that controller accepts the command. H1-2 has no supported sitting or dancing in remote V1.0; do not use H1 L2+LEFT or L1+Y.

Before shutdown, restore tension in the approved suspension, maintain stationary support and enter damping under the operator procedure. H1-2’s own shutdown chapter requires suspension; confirm the supplied pack’s exact off instruction because the paragraph is ambiguous. Do not substitute H1’s battery cutoff timing. Confirm power off before removing packs, cleaning or packing. Do not rely on a suspended damping state indefinitely: it is still powered and needs supervision.

| Remote has charge but no robot control       | Verify actual ID binding, R1/R3 link indicators, radio environment and current control ownership. Low-level mode can invalidate remote commands. Suspend and use the approved hardware/operator recovery; do not test stop authority by letting the robot stand unsupported. |
| Remote full LEDs appear inconsistent         | R3 full is all on; R1 full is all off. Match the physical remote and own-model accessory revision before replacing batteries or assuming a failed charger.                                                                                                                   |
| App cannot discover/bind                     | Check enterprise credentials, Bluetooth permission, proximity and existing owner. Owner-only unbinding is in robot settings; lost accounts go to Unitree. Avoid special characters/spaces in AP configuration as the manual advises; confirm app-version UI differences.     |
| State subscriber times out                   | Verify the approved network route, explicit host interface, domain 0 applicability and model-specific schema/edition. A visible app or working Wi-Fi does not prove low-state DDS access. No mode switch is justified by timeout alone.                                      |
| State parses but joint count is wrong        | HG 35 is capacity; active H1-2 indices are 0–26. Wrong decoder/firmware/coordinate mode must be resolved before using logged positions.                                                                                                                                      |
| Reading code asks for GO voltage/BMS fields  | H1-2 uses HG LowState\_, which has no GO power\_v/power\_a or BMS member. Do not invent a battery reading or switch decoders merely to get those fields.                                                                                                                     |
| Unexpected protective braking or instability | Preserve the approved rig and clearance; manufacturer fall protection is not safety certification. Damping can allow collapse. Remove the commissioning cause under supplier guidance; repeated restart/calibration attempts are not recovery validation.                    |
| Need sitting/dancing/stairs                  | Remote V1.0 explicitly excludes H1-2 sitting/dancing and current stair use; the older manual speed claim conflicts with the current table. Do not import H1 features.                                                                                                        |
| Battery protection / firmware update failure | Use the H1-2-specific supplied battery/firmware instruction and support; H1 protection-flash codes and forced cutoff timing are not established for this pack. No verified controller recovery image, rollback or bootloader procedure was established.                      |

H1-2’s own user-manual scan 20 gives a remote-failure shutdown: after securing the approved frame, suspending both feet and preserving at least 2 m clearance, briefly press both pack buttons together, then hold longer than 2 s. This bounded instruction is distinct from scan 18’s ambiguous normal-off wording; H1’s >15 s forced cutoff is not established for H1-2. If an approved, effective stop/power-removal path is not available, postpone hardware commissioning.
