### Description

CubeMars has newly launched the SW and DW series underwater thrusters, designed to provide clean, convenient, and efficient propulsion power for surface and underwater unmanned vehicles.

The SW and DW series thrusters feature a sealed design, with critical components housed within a sealed casing, effectively preventing damage from sand and other debris. This design enables them to handle complex underwater environments, meeting the requirements for long life, corrosion resistance, and maintenance-free operation.

The thrusters incorporate comprehensive protection, while also addressing the issue of heat dissipation during operation. They are filled with highly efficient thermal conductive materials, allowing them to dissipate heat through water immersion, achieving efficient performance.

The thrusters' sealed casings are sturdy and durable, with a maximum operating depth of 350 meters and a maximum bollard thrust ranging from 7 to 25 kgf, making them suitable for various levels of ROVs, unmanned boats, surfboards, and other surface and underwater applications.
