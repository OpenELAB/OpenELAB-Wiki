### Description

The AK40-10, AK45-10, and AK45-36 series are high-performance power modules that integrate a brushless motor, planetary gearbox, and driver into a single unit, providing the perfect solution for powerful motion and precise control. These modules feature a lightweight design with high efficiency and strong power, offering exceptional torque density to meet various high-load demands. The core components of the gearbox and the manufacturing process have been specially optimized to deliver a smoother operating experience. Supporting both servo and motion control modes, they incorporate adaptive PID settings, eliminating complexity and enabling direct, efficient operation.

The AK40-10 and AK45-10 power modules use a 10:1 reduction ratio, making them suitable for applications with limited space and moderate torque requirements. In contrast, the AK45-36 power module features a 36:1 reduction ratio, delivering greater torque output for heavy-load applications. The plug-and-play, highly integrated design removes traditional redundant wiring, streamlining the installation process and simplifying deployment.CubeMars offers highly customizable modular motors to meet diverse application needs, making them the ideal choice for industries like industrial automation and robotics.
