### Description

The AK40-10, AK45-10, and AK45-36 series are high-performance power modules that integrate a brushless motor, planetary gearbox, and driver into a single unit, providing an optimal solution for powerful motion and precise control. These modules feature a lightweight design with high efficiency and exceptional torque density, making them ideal for high-load applications. The gearbox's core components and manufacturing process have been specially optimized to ensure ultra-smooth operation. Supporting both servo and motion control modes, they incorporate adaptive PID settings, eliminating complexity and enabling direct, hassle-free operation.

The AK40-10 and AK45-10 power modules utilize a 10:1 reduction ratio, making them suitable for applications with limited space and moderate torque requirements. In contrast, the AK45-36 power module features a 36:1 reduction ratio, delivering higher torque output for heavy-load applications. With a highly integrated, plug-and-play design, these modules eliminate excessive wiring, streamline installation, and simplify deployment.

CubeMars offers highly customizable modular motors to meet diverse application needs, making them an ideal choice for industrial automation, robotics, and beyond.
