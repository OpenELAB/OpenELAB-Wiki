### Description

**CubeMars' AKE90-8 KV35 Quasi Direct Drive** is a top-tier solution for **industrial automation and robotics**, integrating a **high-performance motor and precision planetary gearbox**. **Designed for demanding applications** like exoskeletons, bipedal robots, robotic arms, and industrial collaborative robots, it features **optimized gearbox components**, delivering **higher load capacity** and **lighter weight**.

The actuator achieves a **remarkably low backlash of just 9 arcminutes** and an **outstanding torque density of 121 N·m/kg**. Its **modular design** allows for **easy installation and maintenance**, while the motor base includes **threaded and through holes for flexible, bi-directional mounting**, meeting diverse installation needs.

**CubeMars’ commitment to innovation and quality** ensures each product meets the highest standards, supported by advanced manufacturing and rigorous quality control.
