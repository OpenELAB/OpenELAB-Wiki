### Description

CubeMars AKA Series – Heavy-Duty Actuator for AGVs & Wheeled Robots**Built for Strength. Engineered for Reliability.**

The **CubeMars AKA Series** is purpose-built for **high radial load applications** like **wheeled robots**, **AGVs**, and **mobile platforms**. Featuring a **new structural design**, it delivers **over 120% higher radial load capacity** than previous models—capable of withstanding up to **50kg** of radial force .

To enhance durability and simplify integration, the series uses a **robust 2+5PIN plug** that combines signal and power lines in one secure connector—reinforced with screws for long-term reliability. With **one-click motor parameter recognition** and **upgraded driver functionality**, the AKA Series makes **debugging and maintenance** faster and easier.

### Features

* **High Radial Load Capacity** – Supports up to **50kg**, ideal for heavy-duty motion systems
* **Optimized for AGVs & Wheeled Robots** – Designed for mobile platforms and industrial automation
* **Upgraded Drive System** – Enables **one-click motor recognition** for fast setup
* **Durable 2+5PIN Plug** – Combines signal + power, secured with screws for rugged use
* **Easy Maintenance** – Simplified debugging and parameter tuning
* **New Structural Design** – Reinforced for high-load endurance
* **AKA Planetary Reduction Module: AKA10-9** – Compact, powerful, and efficient
* **New Plug Design** – Sturdy, vibration-resistant, and built for industrial environments
