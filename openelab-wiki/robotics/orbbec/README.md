# Orbbec

Orbbec develops 3D cameras and depth-sensing technology for machines that need to understand their surroundings. Its cameras provide depth data for robot perception, close-range manipulation, spatial measurement, and interactive systems.

[Browse Orbbec products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/orbbec)

### Product range

* Gemini stereo depth-camera families
* Femto depth cameras and RGB-D sensing platforms
* USB and embedded camera options, including rugged GMSL models

### Popular applications

* Give mobile robots depth information for obstacle detection.
* Add close-range perception to robotic wrists and grasping systems.
* Develop 3D measurement, people sensing, and interactive applications.
