### 1. Product Brief

#### Gemini 2

The compact Gemini 2 3D camera is based on Active Stereo IR technology and uses Orbbec's custom ASIC for high-quality depth processing. It integrates an IMU and uses a single USB 3.0 cable for power and connectivity. Its wide field of view and depth-sensing range suit robotics and other applications. It operates with the Orbbec SDK in lighting conditions from complete darkness to semi-outdoor environments.

Key features:

* Wide depth field of view: 91° horizontal x 66° vertical
* Depth output range: 0.15 m to 10 m
* Up to 30 fps at 1280 x 800 depth resolution
* USB Type-C power and data connection
* Multi-camera synchronization and 6-DoF IMU

#### Gemini 2L

The Gemini 2L is based on Active Stereo IR technology with a 100 mm baseline and Orbbec's custom ASIC for high-quality depth processing. Its diagonal depth field of view reaches 101°. A proximity sensor provides close-range single-point ranging to eliminate the depth blind spot across the 0-10 m measurement range, and the integrated IMU supports motion and position tracking. The camera uses one USB 3.0 cable for power and connectivity and operates with the Orbbec SDK from complete darkness to semi-outdoor environments.

Key features:

* Global shutters for both RGB and IR cameras
* Wide depth field of view: 91° horizontal x 66° vertical
* Depth output range: 0.25 m to 10 m
* Up to 60 fps at 640 x 400 and 30 fps at 1280 x 800 depth resolution
* USB Type-C power and data connection
* Multi-camera synchronization, proximity sensor, and 6-DoF IMU

### 3. Product Information and Interfaces

#### Gemini 2

The camera provides a USB Type-C interface for data and power plus an 8-pin multi-camera synchronization interface. The front assembly includes the stereo IR sensors, color sensor, IR projector, and supporting depth-processing components.

#### Gemini 2L

The camera provides a USB Type-C interface for data transmission and power plus an 8-pin multi-camera synchronization interface. Its front assembly includes the stereo IR sensors, global-shutter color sensor, projector, proximity sensor, and supporting depth-processing components.

### 4. Software Development Kit (SDK)

Orbbec SDK is a modular platform for camera setup and operation. It supports device discovery and control, device configuration, data-stream acquisition and processing, RGB-D registration, frame synchronization, point-cloud acquisition, and validation with Orbbec Viewer. The SDK runs on multiple desktop and embedded platforms; consult the current SDK documentation for supported operating systems and versions.

The SDK can also report the temperature of core camera components, including the laser, IR sensor, and IMU sensor, through API commands.

### 5. Camera Setup and Operation

#### Packing List

* Gemini 2: camera and USB Type-C to USB Type-C data/power cable
* Gemini 2L: package contents depend on box or bulk configuration and can include the camera, USB cable, and tripod

#### Initialization

1. Connect the camera to the host PC with the supplied USB cable.
2. Install the current Orbbec SDK and launch Orbbec Viewer.
3. For Gemini 2, validate the Unbinned Dense Default depth stream at 1280 x 800, the RGB stream at 1920 x 1080, and the IMU stream.
4. For Gemini 2L, validate the Unbinned Dense Default depth stream at 1280 x 800, the RGB stream at 1280 x 800, and the IMU stream.
5. If the camera is not detected or does not respond, unplug all camera cables and reconnect them to the host to reset the camera state.

### 6. Installation Guide

Using the camera outside the specified operating conditions can cause failure or incorrect operation. These limits apply to the environment immediately around the device under all operating conditions. When the camera is installed in an external enclosure, use active thermal control or another cooling method when necessary to keep the device within its specified range.

#### Installation Recommendations

1. When an external housing is used for dust protection, place foam inserts or rubber gaskets between the camera front and the housing.
2. Do not apply external force to the camera chassis during installation.
3. Do not disassemble the chassis or mounting brackets; doing so voids the warranty.

#### Heat Dissipation

1. Keep direct heat sources away from the camera.
2. Maximize free space inside an external housing to reduce operating temperature.

#### Front-Cover Transmittance

Before changing the camera's structural design, ensure that any protective lens installed in front of the camera meets these transmittance requirements:

| Optical band       | Gemini 2 | Gemini 2L |
| ------------------ | -------- | --------- |
| RGB, 420-650 nm    | ≥91%     | ≥91%      |
| IR/LDM, 858 ±20 nm | ≥92%     | ≥91%      |
| LDP, 940 ±30 nm    | ≥92%     | ≥91%      |

#### Cable Design

Use the included USB Type-C cable. If a longer cable is required, use a USB-certified cable that supports both power and data. A length of 1-1.5 m is recommended.

### 7. Safety and Handling

1. Follow the operating instructions. Improper operation can damage internal components.
2. Do not drop the camera or subject it to external force.
3. Do not modify the camera; modifications can cause permanent damage or inaccurate measurements.
4. The camera temperature can rise during extended continuous operation.
5. Do not touch the lenses. Fingerprints can affect image quality.
6. Keep the product away from children and animals.
7. If the host does not recognize the camera, confirm that the cable supports the required power and data transfer, then reconnect the USB cable.
8. This is a Class 1 laser product. Do not stare into the laser for more than 20 seconds.

### 9. Principles of Depth Field of View

The depth field of view describes the angles observed by the IR cameras. For a target distance `Z`, the active horizontal field of view can be evaluated from the calibrated camera intrinsics:

$$
Z_0 = \frac{B/2}{\tan(\mathrm{ActiveH\text{-}FoV}/2)}
$$

$$
H\text{-}FoV = \arctan(cx/fx - B/Z) + \arctan((width - 1 - cx)/fx)
$$

$$
\mathrm{ActiveH\text{-}FoV} = \arctan(cx/fx) + \arctan((width - 1 - cx)/fx)
$$

Where:

* `B` is the stereo baseline.
* `cx` is the horizontal coordinate of the principal point in the depth image.
* `fx` is the depth-camera focal length.
* `width` is the depth-image width.
* Active horizontal FoV corresponds to the IR horizontal FoV.

Obtain `cx`, `fx`, and `width` from the SDK depth intrinsics. Calibration parameters differ between individual cameras.

### 10. Multi-Camera Synchronization

Multi-camera synchronization can increase scene coverage, reduce blind spots, capture a scene from multiple angles, and increase the effective aggregate frame rate. Both models use the 8-pin synchronization connector. Follow the SDK instructions when designing a multi-camera or multi-sensor network.

Gemini 2 supports synchronized depth and RGB operation in supported topologies. With auto-exposure disabled, the official datasheet specifies a synchronization time difference of ≤3 ms. Gemini 2L can be integrated with the optional Orbbec Multi-Camera Sync Hub Dev or Multi-Camera Sync Hub Pro.

| Pin | Signal           | Description                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | VCC              | Default I/O level is 1.8 V. When a 3.3 V or 5 V drive voltage is supplied, the I/O level can be configured accordingly.                                      |
| 2   | GPIO\_OUT        | Active-high synchronization drive signal. The high interval coincides with IR exposure and can drive an external fill light.                                 |
| 3   | VSYNC\_OUT       | Active-high trigger output for secondary devices.                                                                                                            |
| 4   | TIMER\_SYNC\_OUT | Pulse output used to reset the hardware timestamp of secondary devices.                                                                                      |
| 5   | RESET\_IN        | Hardware reset input. Detection condition: 20 Hz, 50% duty cycle, more than five consecutive cycles; frequency tolerance ±1 Hz and duty-cycle tolerance ±2%. |
| 6   | VSYNC\_IN        | Active-high synchronization trigger input from the primary device; nominal pulse duration 1 ms.                                                              |
| 7   | TIMER\_SYNC\_IN  | Hardware timestamp reset input.                                                                                                                              |
| 8   | GND              | Ground.                                                                                                                                                      |

The pin sequence in the official drawing is shown from the rear of the camera.

### 11. Glossary of Terms

| Term         | Definition                                                                                                                         |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D2C          | Depth-to-Color mapping of each depth pixel to the corresponding color image using the cameras' intrinsic and extrinsic parameters. |
| Depth        | A video stream in which each pixel represents distance from the sensor instead of color.                                           |
| Depth Camera | A depth-imaging module and external interface, typically including an infrared projector, infrared cameras, and a depth processor. |
| FoV          | Field of View; the horizontal, vertical, or diagonal angular extent captured by a camera.                                          |
| I2C          | A bidirectional two-wire synchronous serial bus used to transfer information between connected devices.                            |
| IR Camera    | A camera that detects infrared light.                                                                                              |
| IR Flood     | Infrared illumination used to light the environment.                                                                               |
| ISP          | Image Signal Processor used for image post-processing.                                                                             |
| MIPI         | Mobile Industry Processor Interface, an open specification for mobile application processors.                                      |
| PCBA         | A printed circuit-board assembly containing the depth processor, memory, and other electronic devices.                             |
| Point Cloud  | A discrete set of data points in three-dimensional space.                                                                          |
| SoC          | System on Chip; an integrated circuit that combines the components of a computing system.                                          |
| TBD          | To Be Determined; information is expected in a later revision.                                                                     |
