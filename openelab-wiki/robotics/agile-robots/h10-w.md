## Product overview

A wheeled assistant research platform with a dual-arm torso, perception sensors and onboard computing. The supplied flyer names 3D LiDAR, RGB/RGB-D and thermal cameras; fitted sensors, calibrations and software are configuration-specific.

## Applications

Supervised research, education and controlled demonstrations within a validated operating envelope. The application description does not establish autonomous operation around the public, medical use or unattended household safety.

## Hardware and connections

Battery hot-swap, automatic docking and external power are advertised capabilities, not a wiring or battery-change procedure. Obtain the exact battery/charger ratings, isolation, restraint/stability, stop zones, dock and charging instructions. The flyer does not establish an electrical pinout or a safe speed/force envelope around people.

The exact delivery's actuator/controller topology, joint IDs, power domains, sensor frames, calibration, tool load limits and stop architecture are required. Do not reconstruct them from a brochure image or infer that a Diana arm module makes the entire body compatible with a standalone-arm manual.

## Getting started

1. Keep the system de-energized and mechanically supported as specified by the delivery instructions. Record the exact model/revision, body labels, battery/charger and supplied controller/remote.
2. Reconcile the actual mechanical configuration, arm/hand options, sensor and compute inventory with the purchase/delivery BOM and the specification set above.
3. Obtain the delivered startup, restraint, stop, power and recovery manuals plus SDK/firmware compatibility. Agree acceptance criteria with the responsible integrator before energizing.
4. Prepare a record of joint IDs, coordinate frames, calibration and control authority. Leave walking, base drive, standing, arm motion, docking and battery-changing operations outside this document's first checkpoint.

**Expected result:** a traceable platform/revision/BOM record and resolved commissioning-document requirements. The supplied flyer supports specification review; it does not support an executable whole-body startup or motion tutorial.

## Troubleshooting and FAQs

**A brochure and website disagree:** retain both source/configuration labels and inspect the delivered machine. Do not average the numbers or choose a larger payload/speed.

**Can I use the arm SDK to control the body?** Module similarity does not establish full-body power, communication, timing, balance or stopping behavior. Use the exact platform SDK/contract.

**A battery or actuator fault occurs:** secure the area, preserve the reported fault and follow the delivered isolation/recovery procedure. Do not bypass stops or experiment with unsupported charging or balance commands.
