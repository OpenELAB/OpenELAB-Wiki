### PiPER, PiPER-H and NERO development

The expanded standard PiPER, PiPER-H and NERO references retain their model-specific manuals, drawings and resources, with current firmware-aware Python/ROS 2 development added. They distinguish six- and seven-axis geometry, payload and joint limits, mounting, end-effector interfaces, CAN configuration and calibration. The companion desktop-fixture guide covers the passive mounting accessory and its limits.

Begin with a secure base, the correct power/harness and fresh joint feedback with motor enable disabled. Select the exact SDK/URDF model before trajectory or leader–follower work. The guides separate configuration, teaching, coordinate transforms, motion and recovery, and identify untested hardware procedures and revision-dependent behavior.
