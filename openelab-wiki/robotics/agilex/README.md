# AgileX

AgileX Robotics is a world-leading manufacturer of mobile robot chassis and a pioneering provider of full-stack autonomous driving solutions, renowned for its robust, ROS-compatible UGVs like the SCOUT and BUNKER series. In an exciting advancement for the open-source hardware ecosystem, AgileX has officially partnered with OpenELAB to expand its global reach and streamline distribution. By combining AgileX’s cutting-edge robotic platforms with OpenELAB’s strong community network and supply chain expertise, this partnership offers developers, researchers, and engineers seamless access to industrial-grade hardware and comprehensive technical support, significantly accelerating the development and deployment of next-generation robotics worldwide.
