# RoboSense

RoboSense develops LiDAR and perception hardware for robotics and automotive systems. Its range includes wide-field robot sensors, solid-state LiDAR, and camera-based perception devices for building 3D awareness.

[Browse RoboSense products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brands-robosense)

### Product range

* Airy and Fairy robot-oriented 3D LiDAR families
* E-series solid-state LiDAR and automotive perception sensors
* AC1 active cameras and development accessories

### Popular applications

* Develop robot navigation, localization, and 3D obstacle detection.
* Create point-cloud mapping and spatial-perception pipelines.
* Evaluate camera and LiDAR sensing for mobile robots and vehicles.
