# DOBOT

DOBOT develops robotic arms and automation systems for industrial, education, and research environments. Its ecosystem includes desktop robots, collaborative arms, task-specific workstations, and tools for teaching, vision, and embodied-AI development.

[Browse DOBOT products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brands-dobot)

### Product range

* Magician, Magician E6, MG400, and M1 desktop robot families
* NOVA, CRA, and CRH collaborative robots and automation workstations
* ATOM research platforms, teach pendants, vision kits, and end effectors

### Popular applications

* Teach robot programming, vision-guided handling, and automation.
* Develop assembly, machine-tending, inspection, and palletizing workflows.
* Collect teleoperation data and explore embodied-AI manipulation.

ATOM teleoperation and data kits are optional kits for compatible systems. Workstations, robot arms, and accessories have different scopes of supply.
