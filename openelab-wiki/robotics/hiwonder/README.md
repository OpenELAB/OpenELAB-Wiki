# Hiwonder

Hiwonder develops educational robots, servo systems, and robotics components. Its kits cover wheeled platforms, hexapods, and robotic arms, allowing learners to explore motion control, vision, and ROS-based robotics.

### Product range

* ROS robot cars and mobile manipulators such as ROSOrin and LanderPi
* SpiderPi and ROSpider hexapods and vision-equipped robot arms
* Serial bus servos, mecanum chassis, motor drivers, and expansion hardware
