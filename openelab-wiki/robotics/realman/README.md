# RealMan

RealMan develops robotic arms, joint modules, and manipulation hardware. Its range supports developers working on lightweight robot integration, mobile manipulation, and research into embodied intelligence.

[Browse RealMan products on OpenELAB](https://openelab.io/collections/brands-realman)

### Product range

* RM and ECO robotic-arm families
* WHJ joint modules and electric grippers
* Dual-arm platforms, teaching interfaces, and integration accessories

* Prototype pick-and-place and vision-guided manipulation.
* Integrate lightweight arms into mobile-robot systems.
* Develop teleoperation, dual-arm workflows, and robot-learning experiments.
